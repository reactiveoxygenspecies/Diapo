Editorial

What we set out to do

Every year begins with a question we cannot yet answer. This one asked whether a small team could hold its standards while doubling its output. The honest answer, twelve months on, is mostly, and the exceptions are the interesting part.

We changed how we plan. Quarters became seasons, each with one theme, and every project had to earn its place under that theme or wait.

The second change was slower and harder: we stopped measuring effort and started measuring what shipped. Meetings shrank. Documents grew. The work got better.

What follows is the record: the numbers, the projects, and the voices of the people who did the work.
