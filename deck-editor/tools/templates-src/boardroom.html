What moved the numbers

Drivers

Three things explain most of the change, and only one of them was planned.

  • Expansion in the top twenty accounts: the account team's new cadence paid off earlier than expected.
  • A slower new-business pipeline: two enterprise deals slipped into next quarter.
  • Lower delivery cost: the platform work from Q1 removed most manual onboarding.
  • One risk to watch: support load grew faster than the customer count.
