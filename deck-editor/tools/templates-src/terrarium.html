Part three

A year in four seasons

Spring: sow

Small experiments, many of them, cheap to abandon.

Summer: tend

Pick the few that took and give them everything.

Autumn: harvest

Ship, write it down, thank the people who helped.

Winter: rest

Read, repair the tools, decide what to plant next.

Two ways to grow

Forcing versus tending

Forcing

  • Fast results, shallow roots
  • Needs constant attention to stay up
  • Looks impressive in the first month

Tending

  • Slow start, deep roots
  • Survives a season of neglect
  • Looks impressive in the third year
