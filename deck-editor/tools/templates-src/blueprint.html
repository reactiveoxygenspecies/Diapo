Sheet A-03

What the crossing must do

  • Carry two lanes and a shared path with no intermediate pier in the channel.
  • Clear the navigation envelope of 12 m at high water.
  • Be built from the north bank only; the south bank stays open throughout.
  • Cost no more than the phase one crossing, adjusted for inflation.
Note 1

Requirements in this sheet are fixed by the client. Everything on the following sheets is our proposal and open to change.
