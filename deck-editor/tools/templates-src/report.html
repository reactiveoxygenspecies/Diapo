What went well

  • Volume grew 9% with no added headcount.
  • Unit cost fell for the third quarter running.
  • The new returns flow cut handling time by a third.

What needs attention

  • On-time delivery slipped in August, concentrated in one region.
  • Carrier B missed its SLA in eight of thirteen weeks.
  • Peak-week staffing was short by 12 shifts.

Five things to fix before Q4

Owner and due date in the appendix
  1. 01

    Cap carrier B at 30% in every region

    Removes the single point of failure that cost 1.8 points in August.

    Logistics · Oct
  2. 02

    Peak-week staffing model

    Plan 14 shifts of buffer for weeks 47 to 51 from the Q3 shortfall.

    Operations · Oct
  3. 03

    Roll the returns flow out to North and East

    West saved 8% on cost per order with it; the other regions still run the old process.

    Process · Nov
  4. 04

    Stock-out alerting at 3 days of cover

    The July spike had two days' warning that nobody saw.
