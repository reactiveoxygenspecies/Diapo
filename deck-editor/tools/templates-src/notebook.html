The new flow, roughly

Phone first: for and against

For

  • Matches what eight of twelve people do
  • Forces us to say one thing per page
  • Kills the alert email

Against

  • The four laptop-first people are the loudest
  • Charts need a redesign, not a resize
  • Two quarters, not one
Decision: phone first. Charts in Q2.
